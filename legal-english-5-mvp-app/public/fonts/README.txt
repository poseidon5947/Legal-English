Files were downloaded from fonts.gstatic.com on 2026-09-15 (variable fonts,
weight axis 400-700) and are declared in app/fonts.css. They implement the
typography decision of the Design Freeze Pack v1.0 (D04: Source Serif 4 +
Inter). Serving them from this origin removes the runtime dependency on
Google Fonts (no third-party request per visitor).
